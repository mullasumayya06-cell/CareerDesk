Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026 • Applicable Laws: India DPDP Act 2023, IT Act 2000, GDPR, CCPA • Version: 1.1

Summary for Students: CareerDesk is built on strict data minimization. We only collect basic profile information from your Google login to enable your personal career dashboard, saved opportunities, and application Kanban board. We do not sell your data, we do not track you across third-party websites, and we run zero advertising trackers.

2. Data We Collect & Lawful Grounds

We only collect personal information that you voluntarily provide or authorize via Google Single Sign-On (OAuth):

  • Authentication & Identity Data: Unique User ID (UUID), verified email address, full name, and profile picture avatar URL provided directly by Google OAuth. Ground: Contractual necessity to deliver authenticated student features.
  • Student Profile & Career Data: Optional college or university name provided in your account profile. Ground: User consent.
  • User-Generated Platform Activity: Bookmarked opportunities, application Kanban pipeline stages (Saved, Applied, In Review, Selected), and community event submissions. Ground: Legitimate interest and service delivery.
  • Technical Diagnostics: Anonymized server logs containing IP addresses, browser user agent strings, and request timestamps, utilized solely for rate-limiting, security defense, and uptime monitoring. Ground: Legitimate interest in cybersecurity.

Data We DO NOT Collect: We never collect passwords, payment cards, national identification numbers, physical addresses, real-time geolocation, device contacts, microphone/camera feeds, or sensitive biometric information.

3. Purpose of Processing & No Advertising Tracking

Your data is processed strictly for the following purposes:

  • To authenticate and maintain your active session without passwords.
  • To store and synchronize your bookmarked hackathons and pipeline status across devices.
  • To review and attribute community hackathon submissions.
  • To protect the platform against bot abuse, spam, and denial-of-service attacks.

Zero Ad Tracking Guarantee: We do not deploy Google AdSense, Meta/Facebook Pixels, TikTok Pixels, Hotjar session recorders, or any behavioral advertising cookies. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker student personal data to third parties under any circumstances.

4. Sub-Processors & Data Hosting

To deliver reliable cloud services, we partner with verified, enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure providers:

  • Supabase Inc. (AWS PostgreSQL Hosting): Persistent, encrypted database storage with Row Level Security (RLS) enforcement. Data encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256).
  • Vercel Inc.: Global Edge CDN and Serverless Function hosting for fast, low-latency API and static delivery.
  • Google LLC (Identity Provider): Provides federated OAuth 2.0 authentication. CareerDesk receives credentials via secure authorization tokens without ever accessing your Google password.

5. Data Retention & Right to Erasure

We retain your personal data only as long as you maintain an active account on CareerDesk. If you choose to delete your account or remain inactive for over 24 months, your profile, bookmarks, and pipeline records will be permanently deleted from our active databases.

6. Your Statutory Rights

Under the DPDP Act 2023, EU GDPR, and California CCPA/CPRA, you have the following enforceable rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request a structured summary of all personal data held about you.
  • Right to Correction & Updating: You can update your college name and personal profile directly in the application.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may request immediate deletion of your account and all associated records by contacting our Data Protection Officer or calling the account deletion endpoint.
  • Right to Grievance Redressal: You may lodge a complaint with our Grievance Officer regarding any data processing concern.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026 • Applicability: CareerDesk Services • Version: 1.1

100% Free Core Platform: CareerDesk is free for students and developers. We do not charge subscription fees, browsing charges, or application processing fees. Because no fees are charged by CareerDesk, refund claims against CareerDesk are not applicable.

1. Platform Service Fees (Free Service)

All core discovery features of CareerDesk—including searching opportunities, filtering by category/mode, saving bookmarks, tracking applications across the Kanban board, and submitting community hackathons—are provided completely free of charge (₹0).

2. Third-Party Organizer Fees & Paid Events

While CareerDesk prioritizes free student hackathons and contests, certain third-party events listed on the platform may charge entry or participation fees set independently by the organizer (e.g., college tech fest fees or conference passes):

  • Direct Payment to Organizers: Any fees paid for external events are processed directly through external third-party portals (such as Devfolio, Unstop, Razorpay, or university portals). CareerDesk is never a party to financial transactions between participants and third-party event organizers.
  • External Refund Requests: Requests for refunds, cancellations, or ticket transfers must be addressed directly to the designated organizer of that specific event in accordance with their respective refund terms.
  • Zero Financial Liability: CareerDesk assumes no financial liability for event cancellations, postponements, or organizer defaults.

3. Fraudulent Listing Reporting & Assistance

If you discover that an event listed on CareerDesk was misrepresented, fraudulent, or operated in bad faith, please notify our Grievance Desk immediately at sumayyamulla30@gmail.com. Upon verification, we will:

  • Immediately delist and ban the fraudulent listing from the CareerDesk terminal within 24 hours.
  • Assist affected students with listing verification details to support their direct resolution with payment gateways or law enforcement.

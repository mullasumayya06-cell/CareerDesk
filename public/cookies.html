Cookie & Storage Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026 • Compliance: ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC, GDPR, DPDP Act 2023 • Version: 1.1

Zero Tracking Cookies Guarantee: CareerDesk does not use third-party advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or commercial profiling beacons. We strictly use modern HTML5 Local Storage to keep you signed in with Google and remember your consent preference.

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. Local Storage is a modern, high-performance HTML5 browser technology that stores data locally in your browser without transmitting it with every HTTP request, enhancing speed and privacy.

2. Our Use of Browser Storage

We classify our browser storage into two clear categories:

  • Strictly Necessary Storage: Required for the website to function securely. Specifically, keeping you authenticated via Google Single Sign-On and maintaining session integrity.
  • Preference Storage: Used to remember your choice regarding our cookie notice banner so it does not persistently obstruct your screen.

3. Complete Storage Inventory

The following table lists all items stored by CareerDesk on your browser:

Storage Identifier Type Classification Duration Purpose
sb-*-auth-token HTML5 Local Storage Strictly Necessary Session / Until Sign-Out Maintains your authenticated Google OAuth JWT token securely to allow access to bookmarks and pipeline tracking.
opp_cookie_consent HTML5 Local Storage Preference Persistent (1 Year) Remembers that you acknowledged our cookie and privacy notice so the banner does not repeatedly appear.

4. Third-Party Analytics & Advertising

CareerDesk DOES NOT utilize:

  • Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager trackers.
  • Meta (Facebook) Pixel or social tracking widgets.
  • Third-party advertising networks or programmatic ad bidding.
  • Session recording software (e.g., Hotjar, FullStory).

Your browsing of hackathons and coding opportunities remains completely private to you.

5. How You Can Control or Clear Storage

You can clear or disable browser storage at any time through your browser settings:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Clear browsing data → Cookies and other site data.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data → Clear Data.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → Remove All.

Note: If you clear your Local Storage, you will be signed out of your CareerDesk account and will need to sign in again with Google.
